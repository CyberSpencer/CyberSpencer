# Hi, I'm Spencer Thomson 👋

**Founder & CEO at [AI Integrations](https://aiintegrations.tech) · AI engineering · Business automation · Cybersecurity**

I build AI products and custom systems that connect models to business data, tools, and workflows. At AI Integrations, I lead product direction, AI development, cybersecurity, and delivery, helping small and midsize businesses put AI to work in their daily operations.

My work spans hosted model APIs, local inference, AI agents, and the software that turns them into useful business systems.

## 🚀 What I'm building

- **[AiVA](https://www.aiintegrations.ai/chat)** — our AI website assistant for customer questions and lead capture, grounded in a business's website, FAQs, and documents.
- **[Custom AI systems](https://www.aiintegrations.ai/custom-ai)** — assistants, agents, internal tools, and workflow automation built around a company's processes and existing software.
- **Business integrations** — connecting AI to customer service, CRM, order entry, document handling, and voice workflows.
- **AI roadmaps and implementation** — moving from a business problem to a scoped system, then building, integrating, and improving it through real use.

## 🧪 Engineering & open source

- **Agent coordination (in development)** — a shared workspace for coding and business agents, with MCP-based tools, shared memory, retrieval, and task tracking.
- **[HEIC → JPG Converter](https://github.com/AI-Integrations/Heic-Jpg)** — our open-source desktop utility for macOS and Windows, built with Python and PySide6 for local batch image conversion.
